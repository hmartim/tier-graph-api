# T07 — Positive vs. negative polarity

- **Operation under test:** `compareRelationIdentity`
- **Conformance classes:** Core Model
- **Competency questions:** CQ1
- **Executable fixture:** `tier-graph-reference` → `fixtures/cases/T07-positive-versus-negative`

## Ontological property

Polarity is part of relation identity and is **distinct from evidence stance**. An
affirmative relation and a negative relation over the same endpoints and predicate are
different propositions.

## Scenario

Two candidates share source, predicate, and target but differ in the `polarity` qualifier
(`affirmative` vs. `negative`).

## Required invariants

1. The affirmative and negative relations are **distinct**.
2. They are not merged.
3. Polarity is not conflated with stance (`supports`/`refutes`).

## Expected behavior

`compareRelationIdentity` returns `decision = distinct` for candidates differing only in
polarity.
